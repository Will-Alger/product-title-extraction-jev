Labeling guidelines
  • Brand: select the words that name the company or brand that makes and sells this product, exactly as written in the title. Use only the title; don't look products up.
  • Leave out product lines, model names and numbers: DEWALT, not DEWALT 20V MAX. Keep a company suffix only if it is written as part of the name (IDEAL INDUSTRIES INC.).
  • A brand named only for compatibility ("for Delta drill press", "fits RIDGID") is not this product's brand. If no other brand is named, choose No brand of its own.
  • If two readings are both plausible, choose Can't tell instead of guessing.
  • Each title opens with the catalog's brand highlighted. It comes from the seller's listing, not from any model being tested, and it is wrong a few percent of the time. If it's right, press Correct. If not, fix it.
  • To fix: click a highlighted word at either end to shrink the selection, click the word next to it to extend, or click any other word to start over. Keys: Enter save and next, N no brand, U can't tell, R reset to the catalog's brand, ←/→ move.
